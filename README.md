Data Science - Monster Energy Market Analysis

This project looks into the Monster Energy Company's financial performance with publicly available information in the form of Annual Reports.

My goal with this is to look into past performance and compare trends and visualise how they have changed over time.

My reasoning behind this topic of choice is that energy drinks are a popular drink among students and many young adults. Although I can guess as to why this is (nice flavours, high caffeine content, advertising), I wanted to look at the past performance and history of Monster (my personal favourite) and other similar products.
